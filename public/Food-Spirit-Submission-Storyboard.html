Project description · A living story for every ingredient

Every food gets a digital life.

Food Spirit turns a smartphone food photo into an expressive Perxona character that remembers, speaks, and asks to be rescued before it is forgotten.

Photograph it. Awaken it. Rescue it. Powered by Perxona

01 — The problem

Food waste begins with invisibility

Food at the back of a shelf becomes easy to forget long before it becomes impossible to use.

The information gap “What is still here?”

People lose track of ingredients across the counter, fridge, and freezer.

The timing gap “When should I use it?”

Dates and storage context are fragmented, uncertain, or never recorded.

The motivation gap “Why act now?”

Another static notification is easy to dismiss because it carries no relationship.

02 — How we arrived here

A technical constraint became the concept

We stopped trying to make a banana model.

The stronger idea was not a photo cutout or an unreliable instant 3D model. It was intelligent casting: give each food the best expressive performer, personality, voice, and emotional state available through Perxona.

01

First thought: photo becomes avatar

Appealing as a headline, but native photo-to-rigged-avatar creation is not part of the reliable public workflow.

02

The correction: expression must lead

A photograph inside a frame does not demonstrate Perxona. Voice, face, motion, and personality do.

03

The answer: a Food Spirit is cast

The image identifies the ingredient; the app creates its identity; Perxona brings that identity to life.

03 — What did we build?

One complete loop, not a disconnected feature demo

Photo → identity → performance → memory → rescue.

01 · Discover

On-device food recognition

Full-width MobileNet V2 proposes visual labels. Perxona AI refines the text result, while the user keeps final control.

02 · Understand

Condition-aware planning

Food type, condition, and storage produce a transparent useful-by estimate—not a food-safety diagnosis.

03 · Cast

A performer for each food

Bright foods, earthy greens, dairy, comfort foods, and unknown items are mapped to fitting non-human avatars.

04 · Perform

Voice, lip-sync, and motion

The Food Spirit introduces itself in first person through a live Perxona scene and expressive performance.

05 · Remember

A living pantry on the device

The item saves automatically after its hello and remains available after refresh without an account.

06 · Converse

Pantry-aware text and voice chat

Ask what to cook, which food to use first, or how soon to act. The avatar performs the contextual answer.

04 — Who is it for?

For people, not inventory managers

Anyone whose kitchen moves faster than their memory.

Food Spirit is designed for ordinary households that want a lighter, more human way to remember ingredients—without maintaining a spreadsheet or learning a complex pantry system.

01

Busy households

People juggling work, errands, and changing meal plans.

02

Students and solo residents

Small kitchens where one forgotten ingredient can become most of the weekly waste.

03

Families and caregivers

Shared food, different schedules, and no single person holding the whole pantry in memory.

04

Visual and conversational thinkers

People more likely to respond to a character and conversation than a database row.

05 — Why Perxona?

The avatar is the behavioral interface

Without Perxona, this is a food scanner. With Perxona, the food can ask not to be forgotten.

Perxona supplies the emotional moment: a visible body, voice, facial expression, lip-sync, motion, scene, and conversational presence. The reminder changes from “Banana: four days” to “I am Bana Nori—what can we make before I fade into the back of the shelf?”

ExpressionSeen

Facial and body behavior communicates urgency and personality before the user reads a paragraph.

VoiceHeard

First-person speech turns abstract shelf life into an immediate relationship.

ConversationUseful

The character can reason over the current food and the foods already remembered in the pantry.

07 — What makes it unique?

Intelligent casting instead of pretending to generate a model

Each food receives a role—not just a label.

Food Spirit turns one reliable set of expressive performers into many food identities. The cast changes with the ingredient; the personality and message change with its condition; only one live performer is staged at a time for demo reliability.

Dynamic identity

Dynamic behavior

State can become emotion

08 — Trust, privacy, and honest boundaries

Delight should not hide what the system knows

Human correction remains part of the loop.

Stays on the device

Private local memory

  • The selected photo and thumbnail record remain in the browser.
  • The living pantry uses browser local storage—no account required.
  • The user can correct a label and remove any saved item.
Clearly disclosed

Planning guidance, not diagnosis

  • Useful-by dates are transparent planning estimates.
  • The app asks users to check packaging, smell, and texture.
  • Chat sends the question, food details, and pantry summaries to Perxona—not the photo.

10 — Where this can go next

From one voice to a household food ecosystem

The Fridge Council.

The MVP proves one reliable active performer. The larger vision lets Food Spirits take turns advocating for a shared rescue meal—prioritized by condition, compatibility, and household intent.

“Use us together tonight.”

A banana, milk, and berries propose a smoothie because it rescues the most time-sensitive combination.

Lifecycle emotion

Avatar expression changes as the planning window gets shorter.

Meal negotiation

Characters explain which combination produces the most useful rescue.

Shared household memory

Optional sync can coordinate food across people and devices.

Impact measurement

Track rescued items and repeated habits without reducing the experience to a spreadsheet.
